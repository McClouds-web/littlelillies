Common Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Little Lilies.

What are your operating hours? expand_more
We are open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We offer flexible drop-off and pick-up times within this window to accommodate working parents.
Do you offer part-time enrolment? expand_more
Yes! We offer 2-day (Tues/Thurs) and 3-day (Mon/Wed/Fri) options for toddlers and preschoolers, subject to availability. Please contact admissions for current part-time rates.
What is your sick policy? expand_more
To ensure a healthy environment for all, children must be symptom and fever-free (without medication) for 24 hours before returning to school. We closely follow local health department guidelines.
Are meals provided? expand_more
Yes, full tuition includes a nutritious morning snack, a hot lunch prepared daily by our on-site chef, and an afternoon snack. We accommodate most dietary restrictions and allergies.
