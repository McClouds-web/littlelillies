5. Outings and local walks

From time to time we take short supervised walks near the school. Separate written permission is requested for any outing that involves transport.

I give permission for my child to take part in supervised walks near the school grounds. I would like to be asked each time.
6. Collection

Children are released only to the parents, emergency contacts and authorised people named on the enrolment form. Anyone collecting for the first time should bring photo ID. If someone else must collect your child, please tell us in writing or by phone before collection time.

7. Sun and outdoor play
I will supply a labelled sun hat and sunscreen. I give permission for my child to play outdoors daily, including water play in warm weather.
