What we offer

Our Groups

We take children from two and a half to five years.

Settling In

The first step into a routine. Learning to arrive happily, to be part of a group, and to trust the adults who look after them.

Language & Play

Stories, songs, vocabulary and a great deal of talking, alongside purposeful play that puts new words straight to use.

Early Concepts

Letters, numbers, shapes and colours introduced by a teacher in a small group, built up week by week.

School Ready

The year before primary school. Confidence, independence and the routines that make the first day of big school feel ordinary.

Times and fees

Hours and Fees

We are open Monday to Friday, 7:00am to 5:00pm, and closed at weekends. Send a WhatsApp message if you would like to talk through drop-off and collection.

Fees

Registration fee (once off)P200
DAV fee (P400 + P400)P800
Per term, including foodP5,100
By instalment, three paymentsP1,700 ×3

All amounts in Botswana Pula, per child. Ask us for the current fee sheet and the instalment dates.

What to bring when you register

  • Birth certificate  A photocopy is fine, we do not need the original
  • Immunisation card  Up to date for your child's age
  • Medical and allergy notes  Anything our teachers should know
  • Two emergency contacts  Adults we may call and who may collect your child

Joining us

How to Register

1

Book a tour

Call or send a WhatsApp message and pick a weekday that suits you.

2

Bring your documents

If you bring everything on the day, most families finish registration in one visit.

3

Meet the team

Tell us about your child: routines, allergies and what settles them, so we can prepare properly.

4

Confirm the place

Once the registration fee is settled, we hold the place and confirm in writing.

Good to know

  • Registering does not by itself reserve a place. It is confirmed once fees are settled and we confirm in writing.
