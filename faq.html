Everything a Parent Needs to Know

The practical detail, in plain language, before you ever have to ask.

payments

Fees

P5,100 per term with food included, or three payments of P1,700. Registration is P200 once off, plus a DAV fee of P800 paid as P400 and P400.

child_care

Ages we take

Children from two and a half to five years, through to the year before primary school.

restaurant

Meals

Food is part of the term fee, not an extra charge. Tell us about allergies and dietary needs on the medical form.

menu_book

Daily English

Stories, songs, vocabulary and conversation run through the whole day rather than sitting in a single lesson.

checkroom

What to bring

A change of clothes, a water bottle and a sun hat. The full list is in the parent handbook.

shield_with_heart

Collection

Children are released only to a parent or an adult named on the authorised collection list.

Drop-off and Collection

The rules here are strict on purpose. They are the part of the day where a child is handed between adults, and that is the part worth being careful about.

alarm

Arrive in good time

Circle time is where the day is set up. A child who misses it spends the rest of the morning catching up.

edit_note

Sign in and out

Every day, in the log book at reception. It is how we know who is in the building.

badge

Named adults only

We release children to parents, emergency contacts and the people named on your enrolment form. Photo ID is asked for the first time.

forward_to_inbox

Someone else collecting?

Tell us in writing or by phone before collection time. Not at the gate, and not through your child.

call

Running late

Please call. We will keep your child settled and tell them you are coming. Late-collection charges are set out in the fee sheet.

Times and charges

Call us for opening and collection times, and for the late-collection charge. We will talk you through the pattern that suits your family.

When Your Child Is Unwell

Keep them at home until they are properly well, not just well enough to be dropped off.

sick

Keep them at home

Please keep your child at home if they have a fever, are vomiting, have diarrhoea, or have an infectious rash or eye infection.

home_health

Until they are properly well

If your child becomes unwell during the day we will call you to collect them. We ask that children stay at home until they are well enough to take part in a normal day, not just well enough to be dropped off.

medication

Medication

Only with your written permission on the medical and consent form. Hand it to a member of staff, in the original container, labelled with your child’s name. Never leave medicine in your child’s bag.

Everyday Life

Uniform, birthdays, term dates, and telling us when something is wrong.

checkroom

Uniform

A comfortable, play-friendly uniform. It makes mornings simpler and it makes every child look like they belong, which matters more at this age than people expect. Items are ordered through the office.

cake

Birthdays

We are glad to mark a birthday with the class. Speak to your child’s teacher beforehand so we can plan around allergies and dietary needs rather than turn something away at the door.

event

Term dates and closures

Term dates are given to you at the start of the year and repeated in the newsletter. We close on public holidays, and any extra closure day is communicated well in advance. Ask us for the current dates.

support_agent

If something is wrong

Tell us early. Start with your child’s teacher, who can settle most things quickly. If that does not resolve it, ask for the head or the school administrator. We will acknowledge it promptly and tell you what we are doing about it.

Questions Parents Ask

What ages do you take?add

Children from two and a half to five years, from settling into a routine through to the year before primary school.

What does the fee cover?add

P5,100 per term covers school fees and food. You can pay in three instalments of P1,700 instead. Registration is P200 once off, and there is a DAV fee of P800 paid as P400 and P400.

Can I pay in instalments?add

Yes. Three payments of P1,700 rather than the whole term fee up front. We agree the dates with you when you register.

Do you teach in English?add

English runs through the whole day as stories, songs, vocabulary and conversation, within a Botswana aligned early learning programme.

Does registering hold a place?add

Not on its own. A place is confirmed once we have received the registration fee and confirmed it to you in writing.

Can I visit before deciding?add

Please do. Send a note or a WhatsApp message and we will suggest some times.

What if my child has an allergy?add

Tell us on the medical and consent form. We will talk it through with you before your child starts.

Where exactly are you?add

Plot 17051, Kgosing Ward, Mogoditshane. Call or WhatsApp +267 73 674 494 and we will help you find it.

Anything not answered here, ask us. Call or send a WhatsApp message and we will come straight back to you.
