Drop-off and collection
  • Please arrive in good time so your child does not miss circle time.
  • Sign your child in and out each day in the log book at reception.
  • We release children only to parents, emergency contacts and people named on your enrolment form. Photo ID is requested the first time.
  • If someone else needs to collect your child, tell us in writing or by phone before collection time.
  • If you are running late, please call. We will keep your child settled and tell them you are on your way.

Arrivals & Settling In

Parent Handbook
Settling in

Starting somewhere new takes time, and every child is different. We usually suggest a short first day and building up over the first week. Some children settle in days, others take a few weeks, and both are completely normal. Say a warm, brief goodbye rather than slipping away, and call us during the morning if you would like to know how your child is doing.

Staying in touch
  • Your child's teacher is the first person to talk to, at drop-off, at collection, or by phone.
  • We will tell you how your child is settling and getting on, and you are welcome to ask at any point in the term.
  • Term dates and school notices are given to you at the start of the year, and sent out as they come up.
  • For anything urgent, call the office on +267 73 674 494 or +267 39 61 190.

Care & Wellbeing

Parent Handbook
Meals

Fresh meals and snacks are cooked on site each day, planned around a balanced diet that gives growing children the nutrition they need. We cater for allergies and dietary requirements, so please list these on your enrolment form and tell us if anything changes. If your child needs a specialised diet we will discuss it with you individually.

What to pack
  • A complete change of clothes (two for younger children)
  • Nappies and wipes if your child is not toilet trained
  • A labelled sun hat
  • Sunscreen, labelled
  • A comfort item if it helps your child settle
  • A water bottle, labelled
  • Indoor shoes or non-slip socks
  • Bedding for nap time, if your child stays the full day

Please label everything with your child's name. We play outdoors and get messy, so send clothes that can be washed easily.

Health and safety
When your child is unwell

Please keep your child at home if they have a fever, are vomiting, have diarrhoea, or have an infectious rash or eye infection. If your child becomes unwell during the day we will call you to collect them, and we ask that children stay home until they are well enough to take part in the normal day.

We give medication only with your written permission on the Medical and Consent Form. Medicines must be handed to a staff member, in the original container, labelled with your child's name. Please do not leave medication in your child's bag.

Safety
  • Emergency contact details must be kept up to date. Please tell us as soon as anything changes.
  • Children are released only to a parent or to an adult named on the authorised-collection list.
Behaviour and kindness

Young children are still learning how to share, wait and manage big feelings. We guide rather than punish: redirecting, naming feelings, and helping children find words. We do not use physical punishment or shaming of any kind. If a pattern of behaviour is worrying us, we will talk to you early so we can work on it together.

Clothing

We have a comfortable, play-friendly uniform. It helps children feel part of the group and makes mornings simpler. Uniform items can be ordered through the office.

Birthdays and celebrations

We are glad to mark birthdays with the class. Please speak to your child's teacher beforehand so we can plan around allergies and dietary needs.

If something is wrong

If you are unhappy about anything, please tell us early. Start with your child's teacher, who can resolve most things quickly. If that does not settle it, ask to speak to the head or the school administrator. We will acknowledge your concern promptly and tell you what we are doing about it.
